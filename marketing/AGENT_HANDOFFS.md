# Marketing workstreams and specialist handoffs

Use these briefs when assigning Arena agents or human specialists. Each brief has a narrow outcome and a review gate so parallel work stays consistent.

## 1. Brand guardian

**Input:** `BRAND_GOVERNANCE.md`, `instagram/BRAND_GUIDE.md`, live product URL.\
**Task:** review every new claim, visual and CTA for brand consistency and product truth.\
**Output:** approve/revise/block with a reason and publish date.\
**Block when:** an outcome is guaranteed, privacy is overstated, or the live product differs.

## 2. Morocco localization reviewer

**Input:** final French and Darija/Arabic copy in context, not a word list.\
**Task:** check Moroccan usage, warmth, RTL punctuation, gender neutrality and cultural fit.\
**Output:** final copy plus notes on phrases that should not be machine-translated later.\
**Block when:** dialect drifts outside Morocco, Arabic glyphs are broken, or legal nuance is unclear.

## 3. Instagram producer/community lead

**Input:** `instagram/LAUNCH_PLAN.md`, calendar CSV, assets and captions.\
**Task:** schedule, publish, add native stickers/subtitles/alt text, reply and capture themes.\
**Output:** URL/status per post and weekly insight summary.\
**Block when:** no support owner, UTM link, alt text or local review.

## 4. Lifecycle/email specialist

**Input:** `mailing/`, consent model, sender DNS status and segment definitions.\
**Task:** configure segments, templates, test matrix, suppression and send reports.\
**Output:** proof sends, launch report, bounce/complaint/unsubscribe log.\
**Block when:** consent source cannot be proven or unsubscribe/suppression is untested.

## 5. Paid acquisition specialist

**Input:** `advertising/`, approved landing page, budget, analytics QA evidence.\
**Task:** build campaigns exactly from the matrix, launch controlled tests and pace daily.\
**Output:** change log and experiment readout based on activation—not cheap clicks alone.\
**Block when:** conversion event, exclusion, URL or creative approval is missing.

## 6. Analytics/privacy specialist

**Input:** `strategy/MEASUREMENT_PLAN.md`, app analytics seam, consent and privacy pages.\
**Task:** implement only allowlisted non-financial events, validate attribution and produce aggregate dashboards.\
**Output:** QA event table with timestamp/device and no sensitive payloads.\
**Block when:** a payload includes financial, household, support or authentication data.

## 7. Growth strategist

**Input:** weekly dashboard, support themes, creative log and 90-day plan.\
**Task:** decide stop/continue/scale, define the next hypothesis and prevent channel drift.\
**Output:** one-page weekly decision memo.\
**Block when:** data quality is unknown or a test changed several variables at once.

## Handoff order

`Product truth → Brand → Localization → Analytics/consent → Channel owner → Final QA → Publish → Learn`
