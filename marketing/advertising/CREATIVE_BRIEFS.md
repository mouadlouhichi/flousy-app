# Advertising creative briefs

## Shared production rules

- One message, one language, one primary CTA.
- Use the exact palette/font rules in `../BRAND_GOVERNANCE.md`.
- Keep body copy out of baked artwork where the platform provides accessible text fields.
- Use fictional demo data only; label examples.
- Preserve feed crop safety and inspect every placement preview.
- Add subtitles to motion and platform-native alt text to static assets.
- Every Arabic word uses Cairo; use ExtraBold for display and the variable face for support copy, right-align, and review actual exports.

## C01 — Calm plan

**Insight:** starting feels easier when “perfect” is removed.
**Hook:** `Ton budget. Ton rythme.` / `فلوسك بوضوح، بلا ضغط.`
**Visual:** pale background or forest field, one restrained lime accent, and one monthly plan card with three calm steps.
**Proof:** MAD context and `Prévoir · Répartir · Ajuster`.
**CTA:** `Commencer mon budget` / `نبدا ميزانيتي`.
**Avoid:** before/after wealth visuals, red warnings, coins raining, shame.

Deliverables:

- 1080×1350 feed FR and AR.
- 1080×1920 Story/Reel FR and AR.
- 15-second motion version: hook 0–2 sec, three steps 2–11 sec, CTA 11–15 sec.

## C02 — Purpose versus place

**Insight:** one balance cannot explain both intention and location.
**Hook:** `Pour quoi ? Et où ?`
**Visual:** two large rounded cards. Purpose card: target/lime. Place card: map pin/forest. Connect with a simple line, not an equation.
**Proof:** `Besoins · envies · épargne` versus `Banque · maison · portefeuille`.
**CTA:** `Voir comment ça marche`.

Motion: show 500 MAD moving from bank to wallet while the “courses” purpose stays in place. Explicitly label it as an example.

## C03 — Manual privacy

**Insight:** no bank connection can be a deliberate control choice.
**Hook:** `Sans connexion bancaire.`
**Visual:** forest-deep field, generous shield outline, white type and a restrained lime detail.
**Proof:** `Tu choisis ce que tu veux suivre.` / `Pas d’identifiants bancaires à partager.`
**CTA:** `Découvrir le fonctionnement`.
**Avoid:** padlock clichés, “bank-grade,” “unhackable,” fake certification badges.

## C04 — Creator product walkthrough

**Creator profile:** Morocco-based practical living, organization, early-career or education creator with trusted comments—not speculative finance/crypto positioning.

**Brief:**

1. Disclose paid/gifted relationship clearly.
2. Open with a real tension: “I know what money is for, but I also use bank + cash.”
3. Use only a seeded demo account and amounts supplied in the brief.
4. Show purpose versus place in under 20 seconds.
5. State accurately that SmartJib is manual and does not connect to a bank account.
6. Invite people to see the product; do not promise savings or give personal financial advice.
7. Provide raw vertical file, captioned final file and 30-day paid usage rights only if contract states it.

## C05 — Month reset

**Hook:** `15 minutes pour voir le mois.`
**Visual:** three numbered steps and a calm progress line.
**Use:** organic/retention first; promote only if it drives qualified starts.
**CTA:** `Planifier mon mois`.

## Export QA

- [ ] Native pixel dimensions and sRGB PNG/MP4.
- [ ] 72–82 px feed margins; central Story/Reel safe area.
- [ ] Headline readable in placement thumbnail.
- [ ] No broken or reversed Arabic glyphs.
- [ ] CTA and logo not covered by platform controls.
- [ ] Claim and landing page match exactly.
- [ ] Alt text/captions supplied separately.
