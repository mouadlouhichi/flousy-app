# Paid experiment backlog

Run sequentially enough to preserve a readable result. Complete dates/budgets in the campaign log before launch.

## EXP-PAID-01 — Core message

- **Hypothesis:** purpose/place will produce a higher landing-to-activation rate than a generic calm-planning message because it explains a distinct product benefit.
- **Audience:** broad Morocco, French creative.
- **Control:** calm plan feed.
- **Variant:** purpose/place feed.
- **Constant:** format, CTA, audience, budget window.
- **Primary:** activated budgets / qualified landing sessions.
- **Guardrails:** bounce/engagement, support confusion, page performance.

## EXP-PAID-02 — Privacy frame

- **Hypothesis:** manual control framing will outperform a pure “no bank connection” frame by turning a perceived limitation into agency.
- **Control headline:** Sans connexion bancaire.
- **Variant headline:** Tu choisis ce que tu suis.
- **Primary:** landing-to-activation rate.
- **Guardrail:** trust/support question rate.

## EXP-PAID-03 — CTA intent

- **Hypothesis:** `Commencer mon budget` will increase account starts while `Voir comment ça marche` may increase activation quality.
- **Audience:** winning message cell only.
- **Constant:** creative and landing headline.
- **Primary:** cost per activated budget.

## EXP-PAID-04 — Format

- **Hypothesis:** a 15-second product demonstration will improve qualified starts over a static card.
- **Control:** static 1080×1350.
- **Variant:** captioned 9:16 demo using the same hook/proof.
- **Primary:** cost per activated budget.
- **Diagnostics:** 2-second hold and completion.

## EXP-PAID-05 — Language/creative fit

- **Question:** which locally reviewed creative-language pairing produces the best activation quality?
- **Cells:** French explainer and Darija/Arabic community hook in separate ad sets with comparable delivery constraints.
- **Caution:** this is not a judgment of language itself; format, audience and platform delivery may confound results.

## EXP-PAID-06 — Search landing alignment

- **Hypothesis:** intent-matched feature pages will outperform the homepage.
- **Control:** homepage.
- **Variant:** matching MAD / place / privacy page.
- **Primary:** activated budgets per click.

## Decision record

For each test, record:

- dates, exact creative IDs and destination;
- budget/spend and material platform changes;
- tracking quality and consented coverage;
- primary and guardrail result;
- audience/seasonality caveats;
- `keep`, `iterate`, or `stop` decision;
- next test that changes only one major variable.

Never select a winner only because it has the cheapest click.
