# Paid launch checklist

## Access and safety

- [ ] Two-factor authentication enabled for platform admins.
- [ ] Named owner, backup, daily cap and emergency pause access.
- [ ] Billing alerts and account-level spend limit configured.
- [ ] Least-privilege access; no shared personal credentials.

## Privacy and tracking

- [ ] Consent/privacy review documents any pixel, SDK or audience mechanism.
- [ ] No unconsented product-user list uploaded.
- [ ] Conversion events contain no amount, balance, debt, category, goal, transaction, household or identity data.
- [ ] Analytics QA passed on mobile and desktop; platform vs first-party attribution labeled separately.
- [ ] Small cohorts are aggregated/suppressed in reporting.

## Campaign build

- [ ] Names match `campaign-matrix.csv`.
- [ ] Geography is Morocco; language/creative pairing is intentional.
- [ ] Existing customer exclusion is lawful/privacy-safe or omitted.
- [ ] Search negatives and query-review owner are set.
- [ ] Placement previews checked; no cropped CTA/logo.
- [ ] Final URL, HTTPS, UTM and language flow tested.
- [ ] Budget, schedule, bid/optimization and attribution settings peer-reviewed.

## Creative and copy

- [ ] Claim appears in the approved matrix and matches live product.
- [ ] Darija/Arabic has final local approval.
- [ ] No guaranteed outcome, false urgency or overstated security.
- [ ] Web/PWA language—not “download”—unless an app-store listing is live.
- [ ] Demo data only, no real account screen.
- [ ] Alt text and subtitles supplied.

## First 72 hours

- [ ] Check spend/delivery/URL after launch and at least daily.
- [ ] Verify conversion integrity without making premature creative edits.
- [ ] Monitor product incidents, support volume and ad comments.
- [ ] Hide/report scams asking users for bank credentials; SmartJib never needs them.
- [ ] Pause immediately for tracking, claim, privacy, product or spend incident.

## Weekly

- [ ] Read outcome on cost per activated budget and downstream quality.
- [ ] Log every material change.
- [ ] Move budget gradually; do not reset all tests at once.
- [ ] Review frequency/creative fatigue and negative search terms.
- [ ] Publish a clear keep/iterate/stop decision.
