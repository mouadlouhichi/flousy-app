# SmartJib Morocco-first marketing strategy

## 1. Strategic objective

Validate repeatable demand in Morocco for a calm, manual budget tracker and turn qualified discovery into an activated first monthly plan. The first 90 days should optimize for **learning and activation quality**, not follower totals or low-cost traffic.

### North-star behavior

An **activated budget** is a new user who completes onboarding and reaches the first usable monthly plan. A deeper quality indicator is a privacy-safe return to the product in the following week. Exact instrumentation must use the consent and data-minimization rules in [`MEASUREMENT_PLAN.md`](MEASUREMENT_PLAN.md).

## 2. Market problem

People often know what a budget category is but lose clarity because cash, a bank balance, home savings and everyday wallet spending are mixed together. Many budget products also assume automatic bank feeds or foreign currency/language defaults.

SmartJib’s opening is not “more finance features.” It is:

- a Morocco-relevant starting point in MAD;
- manual control without bank connection;
- a distinct mental model: **purpose and place are different**;
- flexible monthly planning in Arabic, French and English;
- a tone that removes shame and pressure.

## 3. Priority audiences

| Segment | Job to be done | Tension | Winning message | Best first channel |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| First-time planner | “Help me see where this month can go.” | Spreadsheets feel heavy; advice feels judgmental. | `Ton budget. Ton rythme.` Start with the real amount and adjust. | Instagram Reels/Stories |
| Cash + bank juggler | “Let me track purpose without losing where money sits.” | One balance cannot explain bank, home and wallet. | `Pour quoi ? Et où ?` Two questions, one clearer view. | Educational social + search |

| Goal builder | “Help me reserve money for something concrete.” | Savings disappear into the general balance. | A small named goal still counts. | Instagram + lifecycle email |

Start with the first three. Treat goal-building as a retention/content pillar, not the only acquisition proposition.

## 4. Positioning

> For people in Morocco who want more clarity around everyday money, SmartJib is a private, manual budget tracker that separates what money is for from where it is held. Unlike tools built around automatic bank feeds, SmartJib lets the user choose what to track, in MAD and in their language, at their own pace.

### Reasons to believe

1. Needs, wants and savings are explicit planning roles.
2. Bank, home, wallet and custom places remain separate.
3. Manual entry means no bank credentials are requested.
4. The product supports Arabic, French and English plus 12 currencies including MAD.
5. The core budget is free without a time limit; eligible accounts can try Pro under clearly stated launch terms.

## 7. Channel strategy

### Owned

- **Website/search content:** high-intent pages for MAD budgeting, cash/wallet tracking and no-bank-connection privacy.
- **Email:** consented education and lifecycle support. Never add account holders automatically to marketing.
- **Instagram:** primary community and creative testing surface; rotate French and Darija/Arabic.

### Paid

1. Start with intent search and Meta prospecting only after conversion QA.
2. Use contextual interest/keyword targeting before customer-list uploads.
3. Retarget only when consent and platform configuration permit it.
4. Optimize to activated budget when volume/reliability supports it; do not scale on click-through rate alone.

### Earned/partner

- Small Morocco-based creators focused on practical living, organization or career—not “get rich quick.”
- Personal-finance educators for transparent walkthroughs.
- Universities, early-career communities and employee-wellness partners after the direct launch message is proven.

Every creator must disclose the relationship and use fictional/demo data.

## 8. 90-day phases

- **Days 1–14 — Foundation:** production QA, claims, consent, conversion events, profile setup and baseline organic grid.
- **Days 15–30 — Message validation:** test calm planning, purpose/place and manual privacy organically; conduct 8–12 short user conversations.
- **Days 31–60 — Controlled acquisition:** launch small search and Meta tests; one variable per experiment; weekly stop/continue decisions.
- **Days 61–90 — Focus:** move budget toward the message/audience pair producing the best activated-budget cost and week-one quality.

See [`90_DAY_PLAN.md`](90_DAY_PLAN.md) for the operating schedule.

## 9. Budget scenarios

Do not spend the full scenario by default. Begin with daily caps and release reserve only after a complete attribution and activation QA pass.

## 10. Decision principles

- Scale **message × audience × landing-page** combinations, not isolated ads.
- Prefer activation quality over cheap reach.
- Stop a test with broken tracking immediately.
- Read directional results only after the predeclared decision window in the experiment log.
- Pair quantitative results with support themes and short interviews.
- Never trade privacy posture for easier ad optimization.

## 11. Assumptions to validate

1. “Ton budget. Ton rythme.” lowers intimidation enough to earn attention.
2. Purpose versus place is understood quickly and is meaningfully different.
3. No bank connection is a trust benefit for a large enough segment, not just a missing feature.
4. French explanation plus Darija/Arabic community content is the right language split.
5. People who activate can form a weekly check-in habit with light lifecycle support.
