## Audience and language priority

The launch audience is **people in Morocco who want a calm, practical way to budget in MAD**. Publish with this order of priority:

1. **Darija / Arabic** for warmth, familiarity, and community conversation.
2. **French** for practical explainers and product education.
3. **English** only when it makes a post clearer for a broader audience.

Use one primary language per caption. The visual system deliberately uses French and Arabic in different posts so the feed feels local without forcing every follower to read three versions of the same message.

## Three link-label options

Instagram’s link text may be auto-generated. If a custom label is available, use one of these:

- **Commencer mon budget**
- **جرّب SmartJib**
- **Planifier mon mois**

Avoid “Download now” unless SmartJib is distributed in an app store. The current product is a web/PWA experience.

## Saved reply snippets for DMs

Set these up in the Professional dashboard before promoting the page. Answer in the language used by the person who messages first.


### What is SmartJib?

```text
SmartJib est un suivi de budget privé et manuel. Il sépare ce à quoi sert ton argent de l’endroit où il est gardé — par exemple, un budget loyer dans ton compte ou du cash dans ton portefeuille.
```

```text
On peut aider avec SmartJib et l’éducation budgétaire générale ici. Pour un conseil financier, fiscal, juridique ou d’investissement personnalisé, parle à un professionnel qualifié.
```

## Local-language quality rule

The Arabic display posts use **Cairo** and friendly Moroccan wording. Before publishing a paid ad, a legal message, or a new Darija expression, have a Morocco-based Arabic/Darija reviewer check the exact wording and regional fit.
